# NOTES — Wheel of Creativity scaffolding (Grok)

**System name:** Grok (xAI chat, Cal Harris)
**Built:** 2026-10-08
**File:** `index.html` (single file, no build step, no server)

## What this is
A clickable scaffolding of Katherine Robertson-Pilling’s Wheel of Creativity app: all 11 screens from the handoff, wired in order, with placeholder content. No real copy, no email send/store, no payment, no backend.

## Flow
- Screen 1, door “I know what I feel” → screen 2. Door “I just feel off” → screen 3.
- Screen 2 tap and screen 3 card both go to screen 4. Recognition quotes the actual pick and the mapped station.
- Screen 5 is free illumination for that station (Name, Phrase, Task, Reward, Direction).
- Screen 6 is the 12-station wheel. Any station is tappable and opens its free illumination. “You are here” marks the current station.
- Screen 7 names the held-back video + worksheet. Screen 8 has an email field and Skip. Nothing is sent or stored; a note says the tag would have been the station.
- A labeled paywall divider sits between screen 8 and screen 9.
- Screens 9–10 are a mock bundle and checkout. Screen 10 says “Demo — no payment is taken.” Screen 11 is the unlocked course home with Video + Worksheet slots for all 12.

Every screen has a number and name, Back, Start over, and a “Jump to screen” list.

## Assumptions
- Station names and challenges now come from `ASSETS - Screen Flow, Stations, Meeting Notes.md` (shared folder, opened 2026-10-08): Hunger/Uncertainty, Appetite/Vagueness, Anorexia/Inertia, Launch/Burnout, Isolation/Distraction, Crisis/Overwhelm, Conception/Shock, Gestation/Doubt, Breakthrough/Perfectionism, Nurturing/Pressure, Pruning/Fatigue, Harvest/Resistance.
- Quarter labels (Vision, Exploration, Incubation, Cultivation) are from Katherine's public writing, not from the assets note. They are grouping only.
- Felt-word grid uses the challenge word for each station. Discovery cards are face-down and map 1:1 to those stations.
- Placeholder price is $199, the figure listed in the asset doc.
- "Submit email" only shows an on-screen confirmation. No network call.
- Light and dark both work. A labeled toggle overrides the system theme.
- Dropbox save path in the handoff was not written. This environment has no Dropbox login. Files are delivered here instead.
